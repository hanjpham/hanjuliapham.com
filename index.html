PhD Candidate in Public Policy · Cornell University

I am a PhD candidate in Public Policy at Cornell University's Brooks School of Public Policy, with research topics in Applied Microeconomics and Industrial Organization.

I'm working on a location choice model to assess funding implementations for rural health care. My other projects include topics such as the use of electronic cigarettes in smoking cessation and patient outcome prediction based on the teaching function. I am also interested in learning how to use clinical natural language processing to predict outcomes.

Works in Progress

Can Rural Funding Fix Rural Shortages? A Location Choice Analysis of US Physicians (Job Market Paper)

I compare rural-focused initiatives in addressing rural shortages with practice environment components, such as Graduate Medical Education programs and positions, in influencing the choice of practice location. The paper's contribution includes a new approach to assessing the effectiveness of policy in influencing healthcare personnel's decisions and addressing challenges in rural care through near-rural implementations.
