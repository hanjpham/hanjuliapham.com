About

I am a PhD candidate in Public Policy at Cornell University's Brooks School of Public Policy, with research topics in Applied Microeconomics and Industrial Organization.

I study healthcare policy implementation and choice models, including applications to physician shortages, Graduate Medical Education funding, and hospitals' teaching function.

Projects

Funding and Physician Supply: Evaluating Policy Implementation Using Choice Models

E-cigarette Use and Smoking Cessation in Consumer Data

with Alan Mathios & Don Kenkel

Analysis of ~420K consumer records (2013-2019) to study cessation patterns among current smokers. Discrete survival analysis reveals evidence that commitment to switch can improve quit success despite limited impact of standalone e-cigarette use.

Teaching Function and Quality of Care

with Sean Nicholson & Colleen Carey

Investigating the components of the hospital-based care team, with a focus on resident training. I use subtle disruptions and data from various sources to piece together an experiment separating the role of teaching function from other components.
