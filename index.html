Han Pham

I am a PhD candidate in Public Policy at Cornell University's Brooks School of Public Policy, with research topics in policy analysis and health economics.

My job market paper, "Can Rural Training Fix Rural Physician Shortages? Evidence from A Location Choice Model," uses a discrete choice model to study physician location decisions and evaluate policies addressing rural healthcare access. I combine structural modeling with detailed administrative data to quantify the role of personal ties and Graduate Medical Education in physician geographic distribution.

My other papers study the use of electronic cigarettes in smoking cessation using purchase records and the teaching function of hospitals using accreditation data. My most recent side project is using de-identified physicians' notes to guess patients' length of stay, motivated by my interest in natural language processing and clinical data collection.
